## What to say if asked "what's the catch?"

There is none. 9 Construction does reconstruction. We work with mitigation shops every week and would rather be the rebuild contractor people already trust.

## Wording to avoid

- Anything about getting more money from an insurance carrier
- "Fight the insurance company" or similar
- Claims that the tools follow, meet or are approved by IICRC
- Promises about accuracy. The tools give starting points and say so.

## Where to share

- Restoration and mitigation contractor groups on Facebook and LinkedIn
- Local restoration association meetings and newsletters
- Equipment distributors' counters and training classes
- Your own estimates and email signature, as a one-line link
